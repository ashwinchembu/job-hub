Cadence — Software Engineer (All Levels)
Official source: https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/solutions/jobs/4679222006
Verified live: 2026-07-24 (official Greenhouse posting)

Location: Remote
Employment type: Full time

Cadence is the clinical AI company automating the treatment of chronic disease. Its Clinical Intelligence takes on routine, high-volume work of chronic care—monitoring patients, surfacing risks, and coordinating action—so clinicians can focus on decisions requiring their expertise. Cadence partners with health systems and has deep EMR integrations.

The Role
Cadence is hiring a Software Engineer to play a central role in delivering clinical care at scale, building within the core of its remote patient-monitoring platform.

What You'll Do
- Design, build, and maintain services for the Cadence platform.
- Improve engineering standards, tooling, and processes on a rapidly growing team.
- Collaborate with product owners, internal stakeholders, and customers to determine the right solutions for the product.
- Work with engineers through a six-week delivery cycle to ship well-designed, architected features.
- Contribute to continued development and scalability of the remote patient-monitoring platform.

What You Need
- 2+ years' experience as a backend or full-stack software engineer.
- Experience writing server applications; Go is a plus.
- Experience in a high-growth, fast-paced startup environment.
- Experience in a cloud environment such as AWS or Google Cloud.
- Experience with relational databases such as Postgres or MySQL.

Compensation
The base salary range is $150,000–$250,000 annually, depending on experience, skills, seniority, business needs, and location. The role is also eligible for equity. Cadence states the role is fully remote.

The application asks for first name, last name, preferred first name, email, phone, city location, resume/CV, future sponsorship requirement, and LinkedIn profile.
